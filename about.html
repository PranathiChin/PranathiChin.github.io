About

I build things, read too much, watch too many movies to keep my letterboxd up to date. Growing up in Florida and going to college in New York, I love a good layover.

Work-wise, I’ve been at Amazon and a tiny startup called Makase where product and engineering shared one brain. I graduated from NYU in December 2025 with a Bachelor's in Computer Science and Economics, and I’m focused on new grad roles in product management or software engineering. Off the clock I’m usually deep in a book or a movie: fiction, essays, whatever’s playing at the weird little theater.

This site is the spillover: projects I couldn’t leave in a private repo, reads & writing when I need to think out loud, and a home page of polaroids of my life.
