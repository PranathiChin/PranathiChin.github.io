About

I build things, read too much, and treat new cities like side quests: NYU on my ID, Florida in my bones, and a soft spot for anywhere with good coffee and a long layover.

Work-wise, I’ve been in Amazon’s logging trenches and a tiny startup where product and engineering shared one brain. I’m on track to graduate from NYU in December 2025, and I’m focused on new grad roles in product management or software engineering. Off the clock I’m usually deep in a book or a movie: fiction, essays, whatever’s playing at the weird little theater. Stories are how I recharge and how I remember what the code is supposed to be for.

This site is the spillover: projects I couldn’t leave in a private repo, reads & writing when I need to think out loud, and a home page of polaroids because memory doesn’t sort itself alphabetically.
